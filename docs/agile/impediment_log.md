## 1. Overview & Objective

The **Impediment Log** is a mandatory Scrum Master deliverable under the BM 321 agile process management model. Its purpose is to:
1. Actively capture technical, architectural, operational, and procedural bottlenecks as soon as they arise.
2. Formulate clear mitigation and resolution strategies with designated owners.
3. Track resolution turnaround time to safeguard team velocity and prevent sprint delivery risks.

## 4. Impediment Severity Classification Standard

* **Critical / Blocker (P1):** Work is completely halted for one or more members; urgent Scrum Master intervention required within 4 hours.
* **High (P2):** Significant risk to deliverable quality or rubric compliance; resolution path required within 24 hours.
* **Medium (P3):** Inconvenience or alignment risk with viable temporary workarounds; resolved during regular ceremonies.
* **Low / Nominal (P4):** Minor alignment nuances; resolved during standard backlog grooming and syncs with zero impact on delivery.
